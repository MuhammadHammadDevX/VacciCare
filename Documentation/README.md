## 🏛 Documented Modules
1. **Public Information Portal**: Immunization guidelines, hospital discovery, vaccine catalog, digital QR certificate validation.
2. **Super-Admin Executive Console**: Master child and hospital registry, scheduler, multi-criteria analytical reports (Overview, Child-wise, Date-wise, Vaccine-wise), parent booking approvals.
3. **Hospital Operations Desk**: Daily queue management, real-time stock and batch tracking, vaccinator staff roster.
4. **Parent / Guardian Health Suite**: Child profiles, automated WHO milestone timeline, hospital appointment booking, tamper-proof digital certificates.
